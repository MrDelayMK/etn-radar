ETN Radar

A public dashboard for the Electroneum Smart Chain: who holds the large balances, which long-dormant wallets wake up, and how much of the legacy supply still has to cross the bridge before the migration window closes.

🌉 Why it exists

Legacy ETN has to be migrated to the Smart Chain by 31 January 2027. Whatever is still sitting in the bridge contract on that date does not make the move.

The block explorer holds every number needed to follow that, but it shows one address at a time. This site keeps the running totals: how fast the bridge is draining, which pace would be needed to empty it in time, and which large wallets have started moving.

🔭 What it watches

  • MigrationBridge balance, daily pace, and the gap to the deadline
  • WhalesThe top 3,000 wallets, re-read every 30 minutes - and every wallet down to 25K ETN from the weekly census
  • SleepersWallets that sat still for months and suddenly move
  • TiersHow the full holder base is spread across size bands, and how each band grows day by day
  • ChainThe ecosystem tokens on this chain and the contracts that were verified in the last days

📡 Where the numbers come from

  • Sourceblockexplorer.electroneum.com, the official Blockscout instance
  • SnapshotTop 3,000 wallets, every 30 minutes
  • Full censusDown to dust balances, once a week - every wallet from 25K ETN up is listed in the leaderboard
  • Bridge eventsEvery transfer out of the bridge since March 2024, checked daily
  • PriceWhatever the explorer reports for ETN, per snapshot
  • Market capsThe top 300 coins from CoinGecko, once a day (What if)

⚠️ What it is not

Not affiliated with Electroneum Ltd. - this is an independent community project reading public chain data.

Nothing here is financial advice. Labels such as "exchange" are partly detected automatically and can be wrong; a wallet moving does not tell you why it moved.

The forecast on the overview is a straight line, not a prediction. It answers "what if the current pace never changes", and the pace always changes.
